# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vue 3, TypeScript, Vite, and shadcn-vue style components using Reka UI, as requested.

## Product Purpose
A frontend design study for an AI presentation editor. Users explore and edit a demo deck, adjust its design, and rehearse a presentation.

## Users
Assumption from the brief: people building visual presentations, and stakeholders evaluating an editor concept.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Interactive frontend mockup. AI actions are clearly identified as simulated previews. Browser-local persistence, editable slide text, slide management, design controls, local deck export, and presentation playback. No live AI, remote collaboration, or server account behavior is implied.

## Brand Commitments
Reference 1 is the primary style influence: a dark creative studio, rounded panels, pale blue controls, layered materials. The user asks for modern aesthetics, smooth glass, animation, and transitions.

## Evidence on Hand
User-provided editor screenshots. Product name Aera and the sample presentation are invented design-study content. No business statistics or customer claims are presented as evidence.
